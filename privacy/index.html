Privacy policy

What we do with the personal data you send us through this website, and what your rights are under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short

We only collect what you type into the contact form, we only use it to answer you, and we never sell it or share it for marketing. This website uses no cookies, no advertising and no tracking.

What we collect and why

Data
Your name, email address and the content of your message, sent through the contact form or by email.
Purpose
Answering your question, preparing a quote and following up on a possible project.
Legal basis
Steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR art. 6.1.b), and our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us (art. 6.1.f).
Retention
Up to 3 years after our last exchange if no contract follows. If we work together, your data is kept for as long as the legal and accounting obligations require.

Who processes it

The contact form sends your message through a service hosted by Amazon Web Services in Ireland (EU), which delivers it to our mailbox. The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record your IP address in its server logs for security purposes; see the GitHub privacy statement. These providers act only on our behalf. We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served from our own site, so no third party is contacted when you browse. If you use the light or dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, or to receive it in a portable format. Send your request to info@satprod.net; we will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is not handled correctly, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, dataprotectionauthority.be.

Changes

We may update this policy when our website or our practices change. Last updated: 29 September 2026.
