About

Building since 2011.

Satprod is an engineering company focused on software, electronics and infrastructure. Our goal has stayed the same: keep your project at a high level of technology, without the headaches.

What we do

An engineering company.

We design, build and maintain technical solutions for companies, on our own projects or as engineers supporting your team. One team covers the whole chain, from the first design to deployment.

01

Software & AI

Web platforms, applications and back-ends, and AI features including model training.

Networks & infrastructure

Cabling, networks, telephony and cloud infrastructure, from the rack to AWS.

How we work

Built in-house.
Handed over openly.

Every solution is developed internally, which gives you and us complete freedom over the technologies, their implementation and the final design.

  1. Affordable

    The latest languages and frameworks speed up development considerably, and your final invoice with it.

  2. Well documented

    Everything we ship is documented so you can use it to its full potential.

  3. Easily maintainable

    We are fully transparent about the technologies inside, so maintenance can be done by us or by your own team.

  4. Highly customisable

    No black boxes, no lock-in. The product is shaped around your needs, not the other way round.
